## LinkedIn

Most AI systems begin with a model. Æ Orange AI Computer begins with a
boundary: a model may interpret and create, but it cannot certify that work
happened.

Orange puts deterministic control around replaceable intelligence. Typed
orders carry scope and risk. Source-backed memory restores project context.
Context Crystal constructs the smallest useful workbench. Models and tools wake
by lease. Hermes executes inside bounded authority. Verification and
hash-chained receipts establish what actually occurred.

The current bounded evidence accepted 10/10 Blue Bench lanes. Context Crystal
preserved required answers and source pointers across 5/5 held-out cases, with
a minimum 1,422.901x operational-context ratio on the tested corpus. AE
Memory's hybrid retriever passed 23/23 held-out cases at MRR 0.9058.

## Reddit / Technical Forum

**Title:** We built Æ Orange AI Computer: a local-first control plane where
models cannot certify their own work

Agent systems often let one model propose an action, narrate its result, and
declare success. Æ Orange AI Computer separates model cognition from
operational authority.

`orange.order.v1` carries intent, scope, risk, and allowed actions. Bun validates
the order, restores source-backed memory, constructs a compact Context Crystal
workbench, and selects the least-action route. Hermes performs bounded work
through explicit leases. Verification returns `orange.report.v1` and a
hash-chained receipt.

The public repository includes a Windows-first LLM deploy, technical manuals,
raw evidence receipts, and bounded benchmark results. We are looking for failed
reproductions, tool-call mismatches, security criticism, and hardware reports:
https://github.com/AtomEons/Orange-AI-Computer
