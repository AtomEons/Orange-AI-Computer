# Æ Orange AI Computer Publication Brief

Æ Orange AI Computer is a local-first AI operations control plane. It separates
replaceable model work from deterministic scope, policy, execution state,
verification, and receipt authority.

## Public Now

- current source under `system/`;
- public manuals under `docs/`;
- selected raw evidence under `proof/`;
- one historical Windows-first deploy ZIP with a tracked hash and bounded
  extracted-payload lifecycle proof.

## Evidence Boundary

Selected 2026-08-28 receipts record a 10/10 exact-path lab suite, a 23-case
memory benchmark, a five-case compact-context benchmark, one bounded Brain MCP
and Hermes delegation, and named recovery runs. Those results are workload- and
topology-specific. They do not establish universal performance, arbitrary
autonomy, studio-quality media, or current health on another machine.

The tracked broad verifier is not wholly green: its public summary reports 227
green test files and one red operational-audit aggregate out of 228 discovered
files.

## Distribution Boundary

The prerelease ZIP published on 2026-08-28 predates current `system/` and Atomic
Orange source. Its proof did not install or launch external runtimes, services,
or models. It must be described as a historical package, not the current source
distribution.

No current-source package, signed current Atomic Orange installer, independent
clean-machine matrix, or general integration claim is published yet.
