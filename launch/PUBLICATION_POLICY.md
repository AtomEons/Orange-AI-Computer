# Æ Orange AI Computer Publication Policy

Public communication follows the same evidence boundary as the code.

## Rules

- Use **Æ Orange AI Computer** as the product name.
- Use internal compatibility names only for exact artifacts, paths, schemas, or
  commands where changing the identifier would make the instruction false.
- Link claims to a tracked receipt and repeat its material boundary.
- Keep current source, historical packages, machine-local state, and future
  work visibly separate.
- Disclose Atom Eons affiliation.
- Publish failed reproductions, blockers, and corrected claims.
- Do not buy engagement, coordinate fake reactions, request votes, mass-message
  strangers, or paste identical promotional copy across communities.
- Pause any claim contradicted by a public reproduction until the discrepancy is
  resolved.

The goal is inspectable engineering communication, not saturation.
