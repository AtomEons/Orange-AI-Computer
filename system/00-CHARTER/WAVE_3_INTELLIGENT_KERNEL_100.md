# Wave 3 Intelligent Kernel: The Hundred

**Product:** AE Orange AI Computer
**Authority:** Operator
**Scope:** Wave 3 Operational Intelligence
**Status:** Binding doctrine; runtime maturity is recorded separately
**Atomic Orange:** Held; the interface is outside this construction focus

## Prime Law

The Hundred are the complete Wave 3 Intelligent Kernel. All one hundred mechanisms are binding system laws. Binding means every Orange implementation, route, model, agent, tool, memory operation, compression decision, and operator surface must preserve them or declare the resulting debt. It does **not** mean every mechanism is awake for every order, and it does **not** mean a written or compiled mechanism has runtime proof.

Orange applies the Hundred through a least-action workset. Each order wakes only the smallest set of organs and laws needed to preserve authority, custody, evidence, semantics, uncertainty, and the requested outcome. Everything else remains available on disk, addressable by stable typed references, and asleep. The kernel is comprehensive; execution is sparse.

## Maturity Law

Each mechanism has an independent maturity state:

- `RESEARCH`: attributed and preserved, but not compiled into an enforcement point.
- `SHADOW`: executes without production authority and records counterfactual outcomes.
- `COMPILED`: has a deterministic contract or enforcement point, but lacks exact-path runtime proof.
- `ACTIVE`: governs the live path with receipts and rollback.
- `PROVEN`: has fresh evidence against its smallest falsifier on the exact claimed path.
- `REJECTED`: failed its falsifier or violated operator law, with the reason preserved.
- `SUPERSEDED`: replaced by a stronger mechanism with equivalence, migration, and rollback evidence.

**Compiled law is not runtime proof. Process start, HTTP success, a generated file, a passing schema, a receipt's existence, or model prose alone cannot promote a law to `PROVEN`.** Binding doctrine sets the standard; exact evidence establishes maturity.

## Provenance Law

Mechanisms 1-48 preserve the attribution of the research lineages from which Orange derived their operational form. Mechanisms 49-60 are recovered Orange alpha-adoption laws. Mechanisms 61-100 are Orange-authored synthesis and runtime laws assembled from the project lineage. Full source records, source hashes, falsifiers, and adoption status remain in the Systems Design Law Registry and its supporting research ledgers. Attribution is not branding, ownership transfer, or permission to extract community-held knowledge.

The flow is logical, not a command to wake all ten organs. The problem compiler and least-action router derive the minimum law set. Kernel invariants remain continuously binding even when their owning organ performs no active computation. Any sleeping organ can be hydrated by typed reference if evidence, uncertainty, risk, or failure crosses its wake threshold.

## Least-Action Wake Law

Every `orange.order.v1` compiles to a workset containing:

- active commitments and semantic checksum;
- required laws and owning organs;
- source, memory, equation, cartridge, and artifact references;
- route candidates and predicted residuals;
- authority, custody, risk, cancellation, and terminal contract;
- wake thresholds for additional sensing, hydration, workers, models, or nodes.

The workset wakes only what is necessary. A simple source-backed answer may require Organ I, Organ V, and Organ VI. A multi-node build may add Organs VII, IX, and X. A learning promotion may require Organs IV, VI, and VIII. The dormant remainder stays on disk but still constrains the route through compiled invariants.

## Hot State Versus Disk Truth

| Hot, scarce, task-specific state | Disk-backed, recoverable system truth |
|---|---|
| Current operator intent and authority | Full transcripts and ChatBackup |
| Active commitments, constraints, voids, and acceptance criteria | Raw sources, ledgers, files, and artifacts |
| Semantic checksum and unresolved uncertainty | Complete receipts and hash chains |
| Current Solar Wave state, owner epoch, lease, and cancellation | Memento lineage and supersession records |
| Minimum proof cut and evidence pointers | Systems Design Law Registry and provenance |
| Sparse Context Crystal / AIR workbench | Memory indexes, embeddings, equations, residuals, and source spans |
| Current route, pressure, blockers, and expected outcome | Party Line history and BuildRun history |
| Tiny AE Pulse phase and changed-state deltas | Capability history, model results, failures, and rollback records |

Hot state never becomes the only truth. Disk truth is not injected wholesale. Typed references, minimum proof cuts, and exact hydration connect them. RAM carries the workbench; storage carries the recoverable world.

## Organ Boundary Enforcement

- Organs communicate through typed orders, reports, references, deltas, and receipts; never through hidden conversational implication.
- No organ may acquire authority, custody, or evidence by inference alone.
- No model may write directly into accepted memory, law, or terminal state without the owning organ's deterministic gate.
- No organ may call another organ awake solely because it exists; wake requires route relevance, uncertainty reduction, safety, or an explicit operator request.
- No organ may hide degradation. Graceful amputation preserves truthful reduced service and a typed recovery path.
- Cross-organ changes preserve semantic CRC, source hydration, owner epoch, and exactly-one terminal outcome.
- The Operator can inspect, stop, retry, hand off, supersede, or reject within the declared authority model.

## Canonical Interpretation
