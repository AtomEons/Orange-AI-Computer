# Wave 4: A 50-Person Company on Your Desktop

## Product

**AE Staff - Powered by Hermes**

AE Staff turns Orange operational intelligence into a complete, action-first
creative and technical company. Fifty distinct specialists remain continuously
present as lightweight Bun actors. They share current project truth, but not
unchecked authority. Relevant specialists lease one of seven hardened Hermes
execution profiles when real work is assigned.

## Flat Company Law

- The operator is sovereign.
- OrangeBrain governs intent, evidence, and terminal truth.
- Navigator is the only standing coordinator.
- There are no permanent middle managers.
- A project may appoint one working specialist as lead for that order only.
- A working lead must produce an artifact and may spawn only separable work.
- Every role must research, design, build, inspect, fix, operate, document, or
  release a concrete output.
- No role may call acknowledgment, process start, or Kanban state completion.
- Review and evidence authority remain independent from the maker.

## Runtime Shape

```text
Operator
  -> OrangeBrain
  -> Navigator
  -> AE Staff Reactor: 50 live role actors
  -> 7 Hermes execution profiles
  -> tools, models, Codexa, and Human Operator
  -> Mirror, Fixer, SwarmSentinel, receipts
```

The seven execution profiles are Navigator, Builder, Researcher, Reviewer,
Visual, Misfit, and Human Operator. They are capability and permission
boundaries, not job titles. The fifty staff roles map onto those boundaries.

Hermes upstream remains pinned and unmodified. AE Staff is built through its
supported profile, MCP, Kanban, API, and gateway surfaces so future Hermes
releases can be staged and swapped without rebuilding the company.

## Shared Project Truth

Every role observes the disk-backed Party Line and receives a signed Project-Now
Capsule containing the objective, commitments, constraints, decisions,
artifacts, unresolved work, evidence pointers, and prior failures. Normal events
return to Navigator. Specialist inference occurs only after an explicit crew is
selected. A company broadcast is explicit and receipt-backed.

## Compute Law

All fifty actors remain live without fifty resident models. The default Codexa
posture admits eight simultaneous model-backed workers under the current 50 GiB
live-memory policy. Tool-only work may run wider. A 70 GiB posture may admit
twelve model-backed workers only after measured promotion. Queue depth does not
discard custody.

## Completion

Wave 4 is complete only when:

1. The registry contains exactly fifty distinct action roles.
2. Every role has a concrete output and falsifiable completion contract.
3. All fifty actors are simultaneously live and observe the same current
   project state.
4. Ordinary unaddressed work returns to Navigator instead of broadcasting
   authority.
5. Navigator can select a sparse working crew and one temporary working lead.
6. All seven Hermes execution profiles are loaded through one gateway and one
   dispatcher.
7. A real role order reaches its mapped Hermes profile, performs work, returns
   evidence, and closes with an Orange receipt.
8. Restart preserves role state, project state, work custody, and receipts.
9. Codexa disconnect degrades truthfully and reconnects without popup windows.
10. Updating Hermes does not require changing AE Staff role contracts.
