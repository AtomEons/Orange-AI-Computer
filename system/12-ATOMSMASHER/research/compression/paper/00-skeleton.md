## Abstract (placeholder)

We investigate the maximum lossless compression ratio achievable on receipt-stream audit logs generated by AtomSmasher 2, a 620-feature compression organism written in Bun. Starting from a 4-stage compound pipeline (AIR fluff-stripping → Crystal Lattice Compression → Mesh delta-dedup → Brotli) measuring 278.51× on a 1.22 MB corpus, we test eight additional compression patterns drawn from cellular sheaf theory, Celtic ornamental traditions (knotwork, annular key grids, triskele, wallpaper symmetry groups), and information-theoretic sparse coding (spike encoding, regeneration-via-determinism). Each pattern is implemented, measured, and reported with byte-exact reconstruction proof.
