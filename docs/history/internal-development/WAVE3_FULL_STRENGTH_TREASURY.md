## Fidelity Law

The original idea remains immutable source evidence. A smaller proof may test it but cannot redefine it downward. A substitute is labeled as a substitute. Rejection requires an explicit falsifier, reason, and reconsideration condition. Supersession requires the replacement to preserve or improve every valued property.
