# Æ Orange AI Computer Systems Design Research Ledger

**Ledger state:** research, not implementation  
**Research pass:** 2026-08-28  
**Runtime authority created by this file:** none

This ledger converts historically documented mechanisms into falsifiable
research candidates for Æ Orange AI Computer. It is not tribute prose, an
operational inventory, or a claim that the product implements any person's,
team's, or community's work.

Names identify sources of prior work. They must not become Orange feature
names, marketing labels, model identities, or implied endorsements. A mechanism
may be tested only through a neutrally named Orange experiment. Collective work
remains collective: a lead, manager, author, or inventor is not assigned sole
credit for a team or institution's result.

## Ledger Law

- **RESEARCH_ONLY** is a pre-adoption status in this document. At the product
  feature-inventory boundary it belongs in research, never in an operational
  bucket.
- **Exact Orange gap** means that no exact receipt is cited here for the
  proposed behavior. It does not assert that no adjacent code or capability
  exists.
- **Smallest falsifier** is the least expensive real test that can show the
  proposed transfer is wrong, unsafe, unnecessary, or inferior to the incumbent.
- **Reject threshold** is a stop condition. Crossing it rejects or redesigns the
  candidate; it is not a target to optimize around.
- Promotion requires a real entrypoint, a declared baseline, authorized inputs,
  deterministic measurements where possible, limitations, and a fresh receipt
  for the exact runtime path. Historical importance and analogy are never
  promotion evidence.
- No source below grants Orange rights to a name, patent, text, dataset,
  language resource, cultural protocol, or community knowledge.

No exact Orange adoption receipt was found for any entry in this research pass.
Every entry therefore remains **RESEARCH_ONLY**.

## Primary Adoption Candidates

### GAD-001: Erna Schneider Hoover - Feedback Overload Control

**Attribution and source.** Hoover's work at Bell Labs monitored incoming call
load and adjusted acceptance so the electronic switching system could preserve
service under peaks. The result was part of a larger No. 1 ESS team effort.
Source: [National Inventors Hall of Fame](https://www.invent.org/inductees/erna-schneider-hoover).

**Mechanism.** Closed-loop admission control protects essential real-time work
by observing load and throttling lower-value intake before saturation.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves automatic admission control based
on measured queue pressure while preserving designated critical orders and
their receipt chain.

**Smallest falsifier.** Replay one fixed mixed-priority workload against the
incumbent and a bounded controller, inject a two-minute overload, and compare
critical completion, rejected work, latency, and duplicate effects.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any dropped critical order, priority inversion,
duplicate side effect, unexplained refusal, or more than 10% p99 latency
regression outside overload.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-002: Kateryna Yushchenko - Typed Indirect Addressing

**Attribution and source.** Yushchenko and the Kyiv computing group developed
the Address Programming Language and Kyiv architecture with indirect addressing,
tree-like formats, and declarative capabilities. Source: [Problems in
Programming](https://pp.isofts.kiev.ua/ojs1/article/view/479/0).

**Mechanism.** Typed indirect references let a system traverse changing
structures without copying content, provided dereference, lifetime, cycle, and
authority rules are explicit.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves typed, resolvable references among
orders, reports, receipts, memory records, and artifacts with bounded traversal
and cycle detection.

**Smallest falsifier.** Build a read-only 1,000-node fixture containing valid,
dangling, unauthorized, cyclic, and superseded references; resolve it through
one neutral graph-reader experiment.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any wrong-target or unauthorized resolution, any
undetected cycle, non-deterministic traversal, or p95 lookup more than 10% slower
than direct incumbent lookup on valid references.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-003: Gladys West - Calibrated Models and Residual Correction

**Attribution and source.** West programmed increasingly refined geodetic Earth
models using satellite observations and corrections for gravitational, tidal,
and other distortions; this work contributed to the foundation used by GPS.
Sources: [U.S. Air Force](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/1707659/mathematician-inducted-into-space-and-missiles-pioneers-hall-of-fame/)
and [U.S. Space Force biography](https://www.spaceforce.mil/Portals/2/Documents/Space_Pioneers/Space_Pioneers_Bios/SF_Space_Pioneers_Bio_West.pdf).

**Mechanism.** A useful predictive model exposes systematic forces, compares
prediction with observation, retains residuals, and recalibrates without
concealing uncertainty.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves that route-cost or completion-time
predictions model host, load, time, and dependency effects and then retain
calibration residuals.

**Smallest falsifier.** Fit a bounded model to an existing authorized telemetry
window, freeze it, and predict the next 100 route outcomes against a last-value
baseline.

**Reject threshold.** Reject if mean absolute error is not at least 20% lower
than the baseline, a nominal 95% interval covers fewer than 90% of observations,
or any missing observation is silently imputed.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-004: Karen Sparck Jones - Collection-Aware Retrieval Weighting

**Attribution and source.** Sparck Jones's 1972 work gave a statistical
interpretation of term specificity that became inverse document frequency:
terms common across a collection carry less discriminating value. Sources:
[Spärck Jones / Robertson IDF archive](https://www.staff.city.ac.uk/~sbrp622/idf.html)
and [SIGIR museum](https://sigir.org/resources/museum/).

**Mechanism.** Retrieval weight must depend on the collection, not only the
query or document, and must be evaluated on held-out relevance judgments.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves an operator-visible decomposition
of lexical rarity, dense score, project scope, freshness, and evidence authority
for every returned memory result.

**Smallest falsifier.** Run the current held-out retrieval set with and without
collection-aware rarity weighting while preserving all other ranker inputs.

**Reject threshold.** Reject if nDCG@10 improves by less than 2%, exact-identifier
recall drops at all, any source-authority gate is bypassed, or the result cannot
be reproduced from the recorded corpus snapshot.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-005: Xia Peisu - Staged Reliability and Diagnosis

**Attribution and source.** Xia led the Model 107 team and designed system,
logic, engineering, circuit, test, and debugging plans. Chinese Academy of
Sciences accounts describe component screening, staged integration, a

conditions. Sources: [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://www.cas.cn/xzfc/202302/t20230210_4874534.shtml)
and [CAS Institute of Computing Technology](https://english.ict.cas.cn/ns/es/201102/t20110217_65174.html).

**Mechanism.** Reliability is designed across component selection, staged
integration, diagnostic observability, operating conditions, and maintenance
instructions.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves a cold-start fault campaign that
localizes component, process, storage, and network failures at the first causal
boundary under constrained host resources.

**Smallest falsifier.** Inject one fault at each of four boundaries in an
isolated Orange start-and-probe path and compare diagnosis and recovery with the
incumbent procedure.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any undetected fault, any incorrect first-cause
assignment, any source mutation, or less than 25% median recovery-time
improvement with no loss of healthy-start reliability.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-006: Henriette Avram - Machine-Independent Exchange Records

**Attribution and source.** Avram led MARC development with Library of Congress
staff, participating libraries, standards bodies, and contractors. MARC used a
communications structure independent of particular hardware and programming
languages, including variable-length fields and community review. Source:
[Library of Congress MARC Development Collection](https://findingaids.loc.gov/repositories/29/resources/6461).

**Mechanism.** Separate a stable exchange envelope from domain content; preserve
unknown fields, authority records, character representation, and governed
change across heterogeneous systems.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves lossless N-to-N+1-to-N exchange for
orders, reports, and receipts across independent clients while preserving
unknown fields and authority metadata.

**Smallest falsifier.** Round-trip a golden mixed-version corpus through two
independent client implementations and one schema migration in both directions.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any byte-significant data or authority loss,
unknown-field loss, non-idempotent migration, character corruption, or
client-specific interpretation of a required field.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-007: Mary Celine Fasenmyer - Recurrence Certificates

**Attribution and source.** Fasenmyer's thesis and papers gave algorithmic
methods for finding recurrence relations for sums of hypergeometric terms,
work later developed in automated identity proving. Sources: [MacTutor History
of Mathematics](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Fasenmyer/)
and [A=B](https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~wilf/AeqB.pdf).

**Mechanism.** Replace repeated case checking with a generated recurrence plus
base cases, while retaining a certificate that an independent checker can
verify.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves that repeated deterministic
sequence checks can be compressed into independently checked recurrence
certificates without reducing the accepted input domain.

**Smallest falsifier.** Select three existing deterministic sequence-style test
families, generate candidate recurrences, and verify them with a separate
checker against exhaustive bounded cases.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on one false certificate, one unsupported domain
claim, checker/generator code sharing that defeats independence, or less than
30% test-runtime reduction after certificate checking.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

**Reject threshold.** Reject if precision@5 gains less than 5 percentage points,
exact-identifier recall falls, expert disagreement exceeds 5% after
adjudication, or any annotation loses its source and viewpoint.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-010: Jeannette Wing - Computational Problem Formulation

**Attribution and source.** Wing defines computational thinking as formulating a
problem and expressing solutions so a human or machine can carry them out,
emphasizing abstraction, decomposition, representation, invariants, resources,
and acceptable approximation. Sources: [Computational Thinking](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~wing/publications/Wing06.pdf)
and [Computational Thinking's Influence on Research and Education](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~wing/publications/Wing17.pdf).

**Mechanism.** Before routing or execution, make the problem representation,
constraints, decomposition, resource model, and allowable errors explicit.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves a formulation stage that records
invariants, decomposition boundaries, approximation budgets, and false-positive
or false-negative costs before an order is routed.

**Smallest falsifier.** Compare incumbent orders with a structured formulation
step on 20 frozen mixed-domain tasks scored blind for completion, scope
adherence, and operator review time.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any new scope violation, less than 10% absolute
completion improvement, more than 15% review-time increase, or a formulation
that cannot compile back to the original order contract.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-013: Kalpana Chawla - Decomposed Complex-Flow Simulation

**Attribution and source.** Chawla coauthored NASA Ames work on powered-lift

[NASA overset-grid paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19940031890/downloads/19940031890.pdf).

**Mechanism.** Decompose a complex domain into locally tractable models, define
boundary exchange explicitly, and validate the composite against observations
or a trusted reference.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves that multi-agent or multi-model
decomposition preserves boundary information and produces the same accepted
result as a trusted whole-task reference.

**Smallest falsifier.** Split one deterministic reference task into three
overlapping subproblems, execute them independently, and compare the assembled
result and boundary traces with the monolithic run.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any missing boundary fact, more than 1% accepted
output divergence, non-deterministic assembly, or less than 1.5x wall-time
speedup after coordination cost.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-014: Hoàng Xuân Sính - Coherent Higher-Order Composition

**Attribution and source.** Hoàng Xuân Sính developed the theory of
Gr-categories, now commonly called 2-groups: monoidal categories whose objects
and morphisms are invertible, supporting symmetries between symmetries. Sources:
[Thang Long Journal of Mathematics and Mathematical Sciences](https://science.thanglong.edu.vn/index.php/volc/article/download/112/70)
and [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.05119).

**Mechanism.** Compositions that are equivalent only up to transformation need
explicit coherence laws; regrouping operations must not change authority or
observable meaning.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves path-independent composition of
three adapters or transforms, including normalized output, error behavior, and
receipt authority.

**Smallest falsifier.** Evaluate both groupings of one three-stage reversible
adapter chain over golden success and failure fixtures and compare canonical
reports and authority edges.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any semantic, status, evidence, or authority
mismatch between valid regroupings, any non-invertible step claimed reversible,
or any coherence rule that requires manual exception.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

aliases, duplicates, stale destinations, and missing identifiers through the
incumbent and a directory-assisted queue.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on one misdelivery, one duplicate delivery, an

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

faults.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on one byte or linkage misinterpretation, one source
mutation, one malformed record presented as valid, or less than 25% median
fault-localization improvement.

**Attribution and source.** Hopper developed early compiling routines and
advocated machine-independent programming in collaboration with the teams and
standards communities around those systems. Source: [Smithsonian Grace Murray
Hopper Collection](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/archival-collection/sova-nmah-ac-0324).

**Mechanism.** Compile human intent into a constrained portable intermediate
form with diagnostics, rather than coupling intent to one machine interface.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves a typed order intermediate
representation with cost, latency, evidence, authority, and reversibility
annotations across two independent executors.

**Smallest falsifier.** Compile 50 golden orders and execute the resulting form
through two isolated clients against the same deterministic fixtures.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any semantic difference, target-specific hidden
field, diagnostic ambiguity, or more than 15% median compilation/execution
overhead without a measured safety gain.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-023: Frances Allen - Semantics-Preserving Optimization

**Attribution and source.** Allen established foundations for program analysis,
optimizing transformations, and automatic parallelization at IBM. Sources:
[IBM history](https://www.ibm.com/history/frances-allen) and [ACM Turing Award
record](https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/press-releases/2006/2006-turing-award-laureate.pdf).

**Mechanism.** Optimize only after analysis proves which transformations
preserve semantics and dependencies.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves differential equivalence between
an unoptimized and optimized order plan across output, side effects, evidence,
and refusal behavior.

**Smallest falsifier.** Apply one optimization pass to 30 deterministic plans
and execute optimized and unoptimized forms against isolated fixtures.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on one observable mismatch, one reordered authority
gate, one lost dependency, or less than 15% resource reduction at equal latency
and reliability.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-024: Barbara Liskov - Behavioral Substitutability

**Attribution and source.** Liskov led CLU's data-abstraction work and later
formalized behavioral subtyping with collaborators. Sources: [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/person/barbara-liskov)
and [ACM interview](https://amturing.acm.org/pdf/LiskovTuringTranscript.pdf).

**Mechanism.** A replacement is valid only if it preserves caller-visible
behavior, invariants, failures, and evidence obligations.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves all transports and executors
substitutable across success, refusal, timeout, cancellation, malformed output,
and evidence-chain cases.

**Smallest falsifier.** Run one common conformance suite against every currently
eligible implementation of a single Orange boundary.

**Reject threshold.** Reject replacement on one status, output, side-effect,
timing-contract, or receipt-authority mismatch.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-025: Adele Goldberg and the Smalltalk Team - Inspectable Live Objects

**Attribution and source.** Goldberg co-developed and disseminated Smalltalk as
a language, graphical environment, and object-oriented system with the Xerox
PARC team. Sources: [Computer History Museum](https://computerhistory.org/profile/adele-goldberg/)
and [IEEE Spectrum interview](https://spectrum.ieee.org/qa-adele-goldberg-on-the-legacy-of-smalltalk).

**Mechanism.** Put inspectable state, behavior, tools, and feedback in one live
environment while keeping mutations explicit and reversible.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves a live inspector for runs, leases,
routes, memory records, and receipts that cannot bypass approval or authority.

**Smallest falsifier.** Expose one read-only object graph, then attempt allowed
and forbidden mutations through the normal approval path in an isolated state
root.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on any hidden state, stale value presented as live,
approval bypass, unreceipted mutation, or rollback that fails exact hash parity.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-026: Radia Perlman - Loop-Free Self-Stabilizing Topology

**Attribution and source.** Perlman designed the spanning-tree algorithm and
contributed to resilient routing and network self-stabilization. Sources:
[National Inventors Hall of Fame](https://www.invent.org/inductees/radia-perlman)
and [Internet Hall of Fame](https://www.internethalloffame.org/2017/11/30/stabilizing-networks-through-winding-career/).

**Mechanism.** Distributed routes need loop prevention, bounded convergence,
redundant paths, and explicit topology epochs.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves loop-free convergence with route
cost hysteresis across an N150/Codexa failover and restoration cycle.

**Smallest falsifier.** In an isolated route simulator backed by live health
samples, remove and restore one preferred path while recording every decision.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on one loop, split-brain authority, more than one
unnecessary route flap, convergence beyond the declared bound, or an
unreplayable decision.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-027: Lynn Conway and the VLSI Community - Scalable Design Rules

**Attribution and source.** Conway developed scalable VLSI design rules,
coauthored a widely used design text, taught the methodology, and organized
networked multi-project prototyping with many collaborators. Sources: [Computer
History Museum profile](https://computerhistory.org/profile/lynn-conway/) and
[CHM VLSI record](https://www.computerhistory.org/revolution/digital-logic/12/287/1613).

**Mechanism.** Stable primitives, composition rules, rapid feedback, and shared
access to expensive production infrastructure let independent designs scale.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves that one declarative organ
specification can generate a schema, conformance tests, receipt template,
operator surface, and compute lease without manual drift.

**Smallest falsifier.** Generate two intentionally different toy organs, then
run independent clean-checkout conformance and composition tests.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on generated-file drift, shared-state collision,
manual patching, missing proof obligation, or no reduction in end-to-end setup
time across the second organ.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-028: Lillian Gilbreth and Motion-Study Collaborators - Human Work Measurement

**Attribution and source.** Lillian and Frank Gilbreth developed motion-study
methods using timing, photography, and film; Lillian extended human-factors work
into management and domestic settings. Sources: [Smithsonian](https://www.si.edu/object/gilbreth-stopwatch%3Anmah_849208)
and [Purdue Industrial Engineering](https://engineering.purdue.edu/IE/Research/Academics/HF).

**Mechanism.** Measure the whole human task, including attention, reach,
interruption, fatigue, error recovery, and satisfaction, not machine throughput
alone.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves a privacy-preserving workflow
study of steps, context switches, approval latency, correction, cancellation,
and recovery for a repeated operator task.

**Smallest falsifier.** With explicit operator consent, compare five repetitions
of one task before and after a single interface change; collect no content or
biometric data.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on unconsented collection, content capture,
increased error rate, less than 15% median step reduction, or a safety/proof gate
removed to create the gain.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-029: Evelyn Berezin and Her Teams - Real-Time Transactional Tools

**Attribution and source.** Berezin designed a real-time airline reservation
system and later founded Redactron, whose teams built computerized word
processors. Sources: [Computer History Museum](https://computerhistory.org/blog/remembering-chm-fellow-evelyn-berezin/)
and [National Inventors Hall of Fame](https://www.invent.org/inductees/evelyn-berezin).

**Mechanism.** Interactive tools require transaction integrity, bounded
response, fault isolation, recoverable state, and an interface shaped around the
operator's actual work.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves zero acknowledged-write loss and
idempotent replay for a critical queue under process, disk, and network faults.

**Smallest falsifier.** Run a fixed queue through one isolated active/standby
experiment and inject each fault after an acknowledged write.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on one lost or duplicate acknowledged transaction,
state disagreement after recovery, p95 response above the declared interactive
bound, or a recovery result not visible in the normal operator path.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

## Indigenous and Community-Held Governance Candidates

These entries have a stronger boundary than ordinary technical research. A
paper or public page can document a mechanism; it cannot grant Orange authority
to use community data, language resources, names, tikanga, or governance
instruments. A synthetic policy test may evaluate Orange's ability to obey a
rule. It does not validate cultural fit. Real evaluation requires the relevant
community's free, prior, informed, and continuing authorization.

### GAD-030: Indigenous Data Sovereignty - Community Authority

**Attribution and source.** UNESCO's Indigenous People-Centered AI guidance,
developed for Latin America and the Caribbean, calls for participatory
inclusion, self-determination, community governance of data, consent, privacy,
intellectual-property protection, and respect for voices, images, knowledge,
techniques, and symbolic and linguistic systems. This is guidance about rights
held by Indigenous Peoples, not property of UNESCO or Orange. Source: [UNESCO
Global AI Ethics and Governance Observatory](https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/articles/new-report-and-guidelines-indigenous-data-sovereignty-artificial-intelligence-developments).

**Mechanism.** Data use is authorized by the people whose knowledge and
relations it represents; purpose, benefit, consent, privacy, intellectual
property, and withdrawal remain enforceable throughout the lifecycle.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves mandatory community-authority,
permitted-purpose, benefit, consent, retention, locality, and revocation fields
at ingest, training, inference, export, and deletion boundaries.

**Smallest falsifier.** Use synthetic records only to test a policy lease; revoke
authority midway and attempt ingest, retrieval, training, inference, export, and
cached replay.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on one action after revocation, one optional or
defaulted authority field, one derived-artifact bypass, or any claim that the
synthetic test constitutes community approval.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-031: Māori Algorithmic Sovereignty - Authority over Algorithms

**Attribution and source.** Brown, Wilson, West, Escott, Basabas, Ritchie, Lucas,
Taia, Kusabs, and Keegan extend Māori data-sovereignty principles to algorithms
as uses of Māori data. The principles remain Māori intellectual and cultural
work. Source: [Māori algorithmic sovereignty: idea, principles, and
use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15473).

**Mechanism.** Governance follows data into algorithms, parameters, derived
artifacts, decisions, and downstream uses; provenance alone is insufficient
without authority and accountability.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves that a data-use restriction
propagates into adapters, models, caches, evaluations, exports, and decisions
and remains enforceable after derivation.

**Smallest falsifier.** With synthetic culturally neutral data, tag one record
with a deny-derived-use rule and attempt every downstream artifact and replay
path.

**Reject threshold.** Reject on one untagged derivative, one decision without an
authority trace, one cache bypass, or any presentation of the policy simulator
as implementation of Māori sovereignty.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-032: Te Hiku Media - Kaitiakitanga and Benefit-Bound Use

**Attribution and source.** Te Hiku Media describes data as cared for under
kaitiakitanga, with use managed through tikanga Māori and benefits flowing to
the source community. Its Kaitiakitanga License is copyrighted and may not be
copied or used without Te Hiku Media's approval. Sources: [Papa Reo](https://papareo.nz/)
and [Rongo's statement of data kaitiakitanga](https://rongo.app/kaitiakitanga).

**Mechanism.** Stewardship, permitted purpose, local processing, community
benefit, and continuing guardianship govern language technology; public
availability is not equivalent to open extraction.

**Exact Orange gap.** Orange has no cited community-granted authority or receipt
proving purpose-bound, benefit-bound, revocable stewardship for any
community-held language asset.

**Smallest falsifier.** Test only a neutral synthetic policy with allowed,
disallowed, local-only, benefit-bound, and revoked requests. Do not copy the
Kaitiakitanga License or use Māori data.

**Reject threshold.** Reject immediately without written community authority.
Also reject on any prohibited egress, purpose drift, untraceable derivative, or
missing benefit obligation.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-033: te reo Māori-English Database Team - Expert Ambiguity Authority

**Attribution and source.** James, Shields, Yogarajan, Keegan, Watson, Jones,
and Mahelona built a large labelled te reo Māori-English database using rules
and expert manual annotation; same-spelling, different-meaning cases could not
be resolved by word-level rules alone. Source: [database paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.09778).

**Mechanism.** Automated labels must expose ambiguity and defer culturally and
linguistically ambiguous cases to authorized experts.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves calibrated ambiguity detection,
mandatory escalation, and authority recording for low-resource language
routing or annotation.

**Smallest falsifier.** After permission, use an authorized held-out set of 100
ambiguous items; otherwise use a synthetic ambiguity fixture that makes no
claim about te reo Māori performance.

**Reject threshold.** Reject if expert agreement after adjudication is below
95%, any low-confidence item is auto-committed, authority is not recorded, or
data terms do not authorize the experiment.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-034: Pacific Indigenous ASR Study - Adaptation Interference Gates

**Attribution and source.** Xiao, Mahmudi, Thieberger, Ambikairajah, Holden, and
Dang report representational drift and catastrophic forgetting during
sequential adaptation across Pacific Indigenous languages, including with LoRA.
The publication does not grant Orange rights to its underlying language data.
Source: [Continual Adaptation for Pacific Indigenous Speech
Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06310).

**Mechanism.** Evaluate adaptation order, interference, and forgetting across
all previously accepted capabilities before promoting a new adapter.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves a cross-adapter promotion matrix
that measures both target gain and regression on every retained language or
domain capability.

**Smallest falsifier.** Use synthetic or separately authorized corpora for two
capabilities; adapt in both orders and compare base, first-adapted, and
second-adapted checkpoints on frozen tests.

**Reject threshold.** Reject if any retained capability degrades by more than
one absolute error-rate point, target improvement is below 10% relative, order
changes the promotion verdict, or input authority is absent.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

### GAD-035: Warlpiri Transfer Study - Similarity-Selected Source Models

**Attribution and source.** Mylvaganam, Ambikairajah, Dang, Sethu, and Szalay
study acoustic, phonological, typological, grammatical, and syntactic similarity
for selecting transfer languages for low-resource Warlpiri ASR. The paper is a
research source, not community authorization. Source: [Which Languages Transfer
Best to Warlpiri?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10256).

**Mechanism.** Select transfer sources by measured task-relevant similarity and
verify the choice empirically instead of assuming geographic or language-family
proximity.

**Exact Orange gap.** No cited receipt proves that source-model or adapter
selection uses a frozen similarity model and beats a general multilingual
baseline on an authorized low-resource target.

**Smallest falsifier.** With community-authorized data, rank candidate sources
before fine-tuning and compare the top-ranked source with a multilingual
baseline and one deliberately dissimilar source. Synthetic data can test only
the ranking machinery.

**Reject threshold.** Reject immediately if community authority is absent.
Otherwise reject if the selected source fails to improve error rate by at least
10% relative to the stronger baseline, the eventual best source was not in the
predicted top three, or selection changes after held-out results are revealed.

**Status.** RESEARCH_ONLY.

## Promotion Record Required

An entry may leave this ledger only through a new, neutrally named experiment
record containing:

1. ledger ID and bounded mechanism;
2. current Orange failure or cost being addressed;
3. incumbent and candidate entrypoints;
4. authorized input manifest and provenance;
5. frozen test, metric, and reject threshold;
6. deterministic result or separately identified model judgment;
7. adversarial findings and known limitations;
8. exact receipt path and hash;
9. decision: reject, retain for research, or propose promotion;
10. for community-held work, the relevant authority and revocation terms.

A passing experiment does not itself authorize product adoption. Normal
operational law, security review, licensing, community authority, and
independent runtime proof still apply.
